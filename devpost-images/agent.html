How the MCP works

Your agent

Summarizes and sorts your unread, and writes the replies.

Inbox Zero VR

Turns it into a game with a unique code.

Your Quest

Open the link and play both rounds.

Back to Agent

It gets what you replied to and archived, and acts on your inbox.
