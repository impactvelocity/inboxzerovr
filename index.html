INBOX ZERO

Clear the noise.

A seated VR proof of concept. Slash an email to archive it, or grab it and send it to Keep or Tomorrow. Nothing rushes you, and every action can be undone.

The demo uses 20 fictional emails stored in your browser. Gmail is not connected in this build, and nothing is ever sent.

Settings

In VR, open Menu to recenter the seat. Holding a thumbstick click for one second also recenters.

Reminders

    Reminders appear here when you open this app. They are not background notifications and nothing is scheduled with your mail provider.
