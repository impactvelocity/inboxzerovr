# Space saber modeling dimensions

Use these dimensions as the source of truth; the generated reference sheet is visual guidance, not scale-accurate CAD.

- Hilt: 28 cm total; main body diameter 4 cm; emitter maximum diameter 5 cm.
- Axial sections from bottom: pommel 3 cm, grip 12 cm, upper shell 9 cm, emitter 4 cm.
- Blade: 80 cm exposed length, 2.6 cm diameter, rounded tip. Total activated length 108 cm.
- Build hilt sections from beveled 12-sided cylinders. Grip has three recessed circumferential rings.
- Separate objects: pommel, grip, upper shell, emitter, cyan emitter ring, magenta button, blade.
- Button is mounted on front only; its raised edge is visible from the side.
- Keep blade and glow separate from hilt geometry. Use an emissive blade material and runtime glow.
- Suggested game asset coordinates: meters, +Y along blade, origin at grip center for controller attachment.
- Materials: ivory enamel, plum grip, silver metal, magenta button, cyan emission.
